How we work

A clear path from
“what if” to working.

01 / Understand

Map the real process.

Walk through the workflow together: inputs, decisions, bottlenecks, and exceptions. Choose a focused starting point and define what success looks like.

02 / Build

Turn the rules into a system.

Design and implement a tailored solution. Identify the data it needs, the tools it should work with, and the points where your team reviews the output.

03 / Refine

Test it against real work.

Evaluate representative scenarios, improve the workflow with your team’s feedback, and document how to use and maintain the system.

The people behind the work

Shant Tchakerian
Co-Founder | Verdugo Labs

Shant Tchakerian is a technology attorney, entrepreneur, and business advisor with more than 20 years of experience working at the intersection of technology, law, and business.

His career includes engineering and legal roles at Microsoft, Apple, and Netflix, where he worked closely with engineers, product teams, and business leaders to understand complex technologies and solve practical problems.

Shant has also founded and operated his own professional-services businesses, giving him firsthand experience with the challenges of serving clients, managing workflows, and building efficient operations.

At Verdugo Labs, Shant brings these experiences together to help professional-services firms take advantage of artificial intelligence. His approach starts with understanding how a business actually works, identifying opportunities for improvement, and implementing practical solutions that complement the expertise of the people using them.
